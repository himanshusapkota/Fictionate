How is rainbow formed?

Science · 5 min read

Star appear to twinkle because their light passes through Earth's atmosphere before reaching our eyes. The atmosphere contains layers of air with different temprature and densities , which bend the starlight in different directions.

As the air moves the path of the starlight keeps changing slightly. This makes star appear to change brightness and position rapidly, creating the twinkling effect. Star themselves do not usually change their brightness so quickly.

Planets usually twinkle less because they appear as tiny disks in the sky rather than single points of light. Light from different parts of a planet's disk helps balance out the changes caused by the atmosphere.This is why stars often seem to sparkle while planet appear steadier.
