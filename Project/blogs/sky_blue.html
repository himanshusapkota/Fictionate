Why Is the Sky Blue?

Science · 5 min read

The sky appears blue because of the way sunlight interacts with Earth's atmosphere. Although sunlight look white , it contains all the color of rainbow. When sunlight enters the atmosphere, it collides with the tiny molecule of gases that scatter light in different directions.


Blue Lights has a shorter wavelength than red light , so it scatters more strongly when it passess through the atmosphere. This process is called RAYLEIGH SCATTERING.Blue light spreads across the sky and reaches our eyes from many direction, making the sky appear blue. Although voilet light scatters even more strongly,our eyes are more sensative to blue light/


The Sky can change throughout the day because sunlight travels through different amount of atmosphere. During sunrise and sunset, sunlight passes through a greater distance of air , scattering much of the blue light away and allowing more red , orange and yellow light to reach our eyes. This is the why we often see warm colors in the sky during the morning and evening.
